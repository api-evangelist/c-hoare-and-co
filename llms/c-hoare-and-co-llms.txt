# C. Hoare & Co.

> C. Hoare & Co. (Hoare's Bank) is the United Kingdom's oldest privately-owned bank, founded in 1672 and owned by the Hoare family for twelve generations. It is an FCA-authorised ASPSP and regulated UK Open Banking (PSD2) participant, exposing the OBIE v3.1 Read/Write standard — Account & Transaction Information (AIS), Payment Initiation (PIS), and Dynamic Client Registration — through a developer portal at developer.hoaresbank.co.uk. Production access is FAPI-secured with OAuth2/OIDC, PSD2 strong customer authentication, and mutual-TLS using OBIE/eIDAS certificates after third-party provider (TPP) onboarding.

## APIs

- [Account and Transaction Information API](https://developer.hoaresbank.co.uk/apis/ob-account-and-transaction-api-v3-1-0/docs): OBIE Read/Write AIS — retrieve accounts, balances, transactions, beneficiaries, standing orders, direct debits, statements, products, and party data for consented accounts.
- [Payment Initiation API](https://developer.hoaresbank.co.uk/apis/ob-payment-initiation-v3-1-0/docs): OBIE Read/Write PIS — initiate domestic, scheduled, standing-order, file, and international payments on behalf of consented customers.
- [Dynamic Client Registration API](https://developer.hoaresbank.co.uk/apis/ob-dynamic-client-registration-proxy-v3-1-rc1/docs): OBIE DCR proxy — register an OAuth2 client via a signed Software Statement Assertion during TPP onboarding.
